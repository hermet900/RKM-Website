2. Description of RKM2. Kuhusu RKM

RKM is a landlord and property-owner management tool for organizing properties, rooms, tenants, leases, rent payments, payment allocation, partial payments, credit, arrears, expenses, reports, lease events, documents, history and receipts. It is designed for offline-first use. Optional encrypted Google Drive backup may be available when configured.

RKM does not provide tenant accounts, an online rental marketplace, payment-provider integrations, online multi-user synchronization or real-time collaboration.

RKM ni zana ya usimamizi kwa wamiliki wa nyumba na mali ya kupanga, kwa kupanga majengo, vyumba, wapangaji, mikataba, malipo ya kodi, ugawaji wa malipo, malipo ya sehemu, salio la ziada, madeni, gharama, ripoti, matukio ya mikataba, nyaraka, historia na risiti. Imeundwa kufanya kazi bila intaneti. Nakala iliyosimbwa ya Google Drive inaweza kupatikana kwa hiari ikiwekwa.

RKM haitoi akaunti za wapangaji, soko la nyumba mtandaoni, miunganisho ya watoa huduma za malipo, ulandanishi wa watumiaji wengi mtandaoni, au ushirikiano wa moja kwa moja.
