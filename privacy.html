Privacy PolicySera ya Faragha

How RKM - Rent Keeping Manager handles information when you use the application.Jinsi RKM - Rent Keeping Manager inavyoshughulikia taarifa unapotumia programu.

Effective date: 28 September 2026Tarehe ya kuanza: 28 Septemba 2026

1. Local-first information1. Taarifa huhifadhiwa kwenye kifaa

RKM is designed to work offline. Rental and property records are primarily stored on the device where you use the app. Depending on the features you use, those records can include properties, rooms, tenant details, leases, payments, expenses, reports, activity history, documents or media references, and application settings.

RKM does not automatically upload all of this information to a server. The app does not provide online tenant accounts, real-time collaboration, or cloud database synchronization.

RKM imeundwa kufanya kazi bila intaneti. Taarifa za upangishaji na mali huhifadhiwa hasa kwenye kifaa unachotumia. Kulingana na vipengele unavyotumia, taarifa hizo zinaweza kujumuisha majengo, vyumba, taarifa za wapangaji, mikataba, malipo, gharama, ripoti, historia ya shughuli, marejeo ya nyaraka au picha, na mipangilio ya programu.

RKM haipakii taarifa hizi zote kwenye seva moja kwa moja. Programu haitoi akaunti za mtandaoni za wapangaji, ushirikiano wa moja kwa moja mtandaoni, au ulandanishi wa hifadhidata ya wingu.

2. Optional Google Drive backup2. Nakala ya hiari ya Google Drive

If you choose to connect Google Drive, RKM uses Google authentication only for the optional backup feature. RKM requests the scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata, which is intended for application-specific data. It does not request full Drive access, browse arbitrary Drive files, or access unrelated documents.

Backups are stored in the app-specific Drive appDataFolder, not as ordinary visible files in My Drive. Google describes this folder as accessible to the creating application and hidden from normal Drive browsing. RKM lists and downloads its own backup files only.

Ukichagua kuunganisha Google Drive, RKM hutumia uthibitishaji wa Google kwa kipengele cha hiari cha nakala pekee. RKM huomba ruhusa ya https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata, inayokusudiwa kwa taarifa za programu husika. Haiombi ruhusa kamili ya Drive, haitafuti faili zozote za Drive, wala haifikii nyaraka zisizohusiana.

Nakala huhifadhiwa katika appDataFolder maalum ya programu, si kama faili za kawaida zinazoonekana kwenye My Drive. Google hueleza kuwa folda hii hufikiwa na programu iliyoitengeneza na imefichwa kwenye uvinjari wa kawaida wa Drive. RKM huorodhesha na kupakua faili zake za nakala pekee.

3. Backup encryption3. Usimbaji wa nakala

RKM creates a versioned backup file and encrypts its contents before an optional Google Drive upload. Drive file metadata is limited to information needed to identify and manage a backup, such as its format, version, app version, creation time, and size. Tenant and payment records, backup passwords, and encryption keys are not intentionally placed in Drive metadata.

Keep your backup password private. Without the correct password, a backup cannot be restored through RKM. No encryption method can guarantee absolute security.

RKM hutengeneza faili la nakala lenye toleo na husimba maudhui yake kabla ya kupakiwa Google Drive kwa hiari. Metadata ya faili la Drive huwa na taarifa zinazohitajika kutambua na kusimamia nakala, kama umbizo, toleo, toleo la programu, muda wa kutengeneza na ukubwa. Taarifa za wapangaji na malipo, nenosiri la nakala na funguo za usimbaji haziwekwi kimakusudi kwenye metadata ya Drive.

Weka nenosiri la nakala kuwa siri. Bila nenosiri sahihi, nakala haiwezi kurejeshwa kupitia RKM. Hakuna njia ya usimbaji inayoweza kuhakikisha usalama kamili.

4. Google and other services4. Google na huduma nyingine

Google is a third-party service used when you choose Google sign-in and Drive backup. Google's handling of account information and Drive data is also subject to Google's terms and privacy information. RKM does not sell personal information and does not include advertising trackers in this website.

Normal use of RKM does not require a Google account. Google access tokens are used to make authorized Drive requests and are not included in backup files or shown in the app.

Google ni huduma ya mtu mwingine inayotumika unapochagua kuingia kwa Google na kutumia nakala ya Drive. Ushughulikiaji wa taarifa za akaunti na data ya Drive pia hutegemea masharti na taarifa za faragha za Google. RKM haiuzi taarifa binafsi na tovuti hii haina vifuatiliaji vya matangazo.

Matumizi ya kawaida ya RKM hayahitaji akaunti ya Google. Tokeni za ufikiaji za Google hutumika kutuma maombi yaliyoidhinishwa ya Drive na haziwekwi ndani ya faili za nakala wala kuonyeshwa kwenye programu.

5. Retention and deletion5. Uhifadhi na ufutaji

You control local records through the app's supported management features. Removing the app or clearing its data may delete local information, depending on your device and operating-system behavior. Export an encrypted backup before removing the app if you may need your records later.

Disconnecting Google sign-in does not delete Drive backups. RKM does not claim that appDataFolder is permanent or immutable: Google may remove app-specific data when the user uninstalls/removes the app's stored data, and Google account owners may manage application data through their Google account settings. Keep a separate exported backup for disaster recovery.

Unadhibiti taarifa za kifaa kupitia vipengele vya usimamizi vinavyopatikana ndani ya programu. Kuondoa programu au kufuta data yake kunaweza kufuta taarifa za kifaa, kulingana na tabia ya kifaa na mfumo wake. Hamisha nakala iliyosimbwa kabla ya kuondoa programu ikiwa unaweza kuhitaji taarifa baadaye.

Kutenganisha akaunti ya Google hakufuti nakala za Drive. RKM haidai kuwa appDataFolder ni hifadhi ya kudumu au isiyoweza kufutwa: Google inaweza kuondoa data ya programu mtumiaji anapoondoa programu/data yake, na mmiliki wa akaunti anaweza kusimamia data ya programu kupitia mipangilio ya akaunti ya Google. Hifadhi nakala tofauti uliyohamisha kwa ajili ya dharura.

6. Children6. Watoto

RKM is a property-management tool intended for adults managing rental records. It is not designed for children to create accounts or submit information.

RKM ni zana ya usimamizi wa mali inayolengwa kwa watu wazima wanaosimamia taarifa za upangishaji. Haikuundwa kwa watoto kufungua akaunti au kutuma taarifa.
