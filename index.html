Your records are yours to manage.Taarifa zako ziko chini ya usimamizi wako.

RKM is designed to keep rental records on your device so you can work without an internet connection. Google Drive backup is optional and uploads only an encrypted RKM backup when you choose to use it.RKM imeundwa kuhifadhi taarifa za upangishaji kwenye kifaa chako ili uweze kufanya kazi bila intaneti. Hifadhi nakala ya Google Drive ni ya hiari na hupakia nakala ya RKM iliyosimbwa tu unapoichagua.

A backup is your choiceNakala ni chaguo lako

Keep a copy. Keep control.Hifadhi nakala. Baki na udhibiti.

Export an encrypted backup for storage wherever you choose. If you connect Google Drive, RKM uses Google's app-specific appDataFolder—not your regular Drive files—and requests only the access needed for that backup feature.Hamisha nakala iliyosimbwa na kuihifadhi unapotaka. Ukiunganisha Google Drive, RKM hutumia eneo la appDataFolder la programu pekee, si faili zako za kawaida za Drive, na huomba ruhusa inayohitajika kwa nakala hiyo tu.

AppDataFolder is hidden from normal Drive browsing, but it is not permanent storage: removing the app's stored data can remove its backups. Keep an exported copy for disaster recovery.appDataFolder haionekani kwenye matumizi ya kawaida ya Drive, lakini si hifadhi ya kudumu: kuondoa data ya programu kunaweza kuondoa nakala zake. Hifadhi pia nakala uliyoihamisha kwa dharura.
